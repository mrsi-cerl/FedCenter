Technology News and Trends is a quarterly newsletter published by EPA's Office of Superfund Remediation and Technology Innovation (OSRTI) to share information about innovative and alternative technologies and techniques for use at hazardous waste sites. Each issue features articles contributed by project managers and other technical experts on strategies recently used to characterize or remediate soil, sediment, and groundwater at specific sites.
