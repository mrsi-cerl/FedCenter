title: The Chemical Reactivity Worksheet

A free program you can use to find out about the reactivity of substances or mixtures of substances (reactivity is the tendency of substances to undergo chemical change). It includes:  <ul>  <li> a database of reactivity information for more than 6,000 common hazardous chemicals,  <li> a way for you to virtually "mix" chemicals--like the chemicals in a derailed tankcars --to find out what dangers could arise from accidental mixing.  </ul>
