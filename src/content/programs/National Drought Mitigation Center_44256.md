title: National Drought Mitigation Center

The Drought Center helps people, organizations and institutions build resilience to drought through monitoring and planning, and they are the academic partner and web host of the U.S. Drought Monitor. Their capabilities include climatology, social science and public engagement, and they work at all scales, from individual ranches to local, state and tribal government, and countries around the world.
