EPA is committed to reducing greenhouse gas emissions, energy, water and waste at its facilities and operating high-performance sustainable buildings. In addition to demonstrating leadership, these efforts help the Agency meet the following federal requirements and guidance:    Energy Act of 2020  Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (EISA 2007)  Guiding Principles for Sustainable Federal Buildings  Technical Guidance on Implementing the Stormwater Runoff Requirements for Federal Projects under Section 438 of EISA 2007  Previous federal requirements
