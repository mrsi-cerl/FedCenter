title: Turf Trade Tool

The Turf Trade Tool supports water and landscape professionals in calculating the potential water savings and water conservation return on investment (ROI) of a Turf Trade Program. Users can leverage the Tool to estimate potential savings before implementing a program and evaluate expected water savings and conservation ROI on an annual basis.
