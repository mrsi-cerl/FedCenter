<b>Actions Concerning Regulations That Significantly Affect Energy Supply, Distribution, or Use</b><br>  <i>18 May 2001</i><br>  This EO establishes the mandate for federal agencies to prepare a Statement of Energy Effects when undertaking certain agency actions.
