<b>Ensuring Commercial, Cost-Effective Solutions in Federal Contracts</b><br>  <i> 18 April 2025</i>  <br>  This EO states federal agencies to prioritize the procurement of commercially available products and services over custom or non-commercial alternatives. The goal is to streamline procurement processes and reduce costs by leveraging existing commercial solutions.
