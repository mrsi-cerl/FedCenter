The Tabletop Exercise Tool for Drinking Water and Wastewater Utilities (TTX Tool) provides users with the resources to plan, conduct and evaluate tabletop exercises that focus on Water Sector-related incidents and challenges. The TTX Tool web application has been updated in November 2024 and now contains 15 all-hazards scenarios (e.g., natural disasters, man-made incidents) related to emergency preparedness and response. Two new TTX scenarios include Wildfire and Supply Chain Disruption/Emergency Drinking Water Supply. Each scenario has: Fully-customizable situation manuals and after action reports, discussion questions, and PowerPoint presentations
