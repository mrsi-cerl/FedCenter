<b>Planning for Federal Sustainability in the Next Decade</b><br>
<i>19 March 2015</i><br>
The goal of EO 13693 is to maintain Federal leadership in sustainability and greenhouse gas emission reductions. Signed by President Obama on 19 March 2015.  Section 16 of this EO revokes the following:
<ul>
<li>Executive Order 13423 of January 24, 2007;
<li>Executive Order 13514 of October 5, 2009;
<li>Presidential Memorandum of December 2, 2011 (Implementation of Energy Savings Projects and Performance-Based Contracting for Energy Savings);
<li>Section 1 of Presidential Memorandum of February 21, 2012 (Driving Innovation and Creating Jobs in Rural America through Biobased and Sustainable Product Procurement); and 
<li>Presidential Memorandum of December 5, 2013 (Federal Leadership on Energy Management); and
<li>Presidential Memorandum of May 24, 2011 (Federal Fleet Performance).
</ul>
