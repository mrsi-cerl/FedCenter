2011 Revision (Supersedes GAO-07-731G) [Reissued on January 20, 2012]. This document outlines standards that contain requirements for auditor reporting on internal control. While not specific to environmental compliance auditing, the processes and tools necessary for a high quality auditing program are detailed.
