pubDate: 2005-02-04
---
Under READ services, EPA has awarded eight Government Wide Acquisition Contracts (GWACs) to small businesses (three nationwide, three in the eastern U.S. and two in western U.S.). Under the contracts, companies will evaluate each item and its components, and then, in decreasing preference:
<ul><li> refurbish and resell them, using the proceeds to offset costs;
<li> donate them to charitable causes; 
<li> recycle as much as possible; and 
<li> properly dispose of the remainder.</ul>
