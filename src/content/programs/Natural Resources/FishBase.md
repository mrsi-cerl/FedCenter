title: FishBase

FishBase a global information systems on fishes and is a relational database with information to cater to different professionals such as research scientists, fisheries managers, zoologists and many more.  FishBase on the web contains practically all fish species known to science.  FishBase was developed at the <a href="http://www.worldfishcenter.org/"  target="new">WorldFish Center</a> in collaboration with the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (<a href="http://www.fao.org/fi/default.asp" target="new">FAO</a>) and many other partners, and with support from the European Commission (EC).
