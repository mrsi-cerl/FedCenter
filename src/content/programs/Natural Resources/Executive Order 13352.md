<b>Facilitation of Cooperative Conservation</b><br>
<i>26 Aug 2004</i><br>
This EO requires that the Secretaries of the Interior, Agriculture, Commerce, and Defense and the Administrator of the EPA shall:<br>
1. carry out the programs, projects, and activities of the agency that they respectively head that implement laws relating to the environment and natural resources in a manner that:
<ol type='a'>
<li>facilitates cooperative conservation;<br>
<li>takes appropriate account of and respects the interests of persons with ownership or other legally recognized interests in land and other natural resources;<br>
<li>properly accommodates local participation in Federal decisionmaking; and<br>
<li>provides that the programs, projects, and activities are consistent with protecting public health and safety;<br>
</ol>
2. report annually to the Chairman of the CEQ on actions taken to implement this order; and<br>
3. provide funding to the Office of the Environmental Quality Mgmt Fund for a Conference.
