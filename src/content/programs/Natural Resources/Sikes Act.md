title: Sikes Act

This Act (<a href="https://www.gpo.gov/fdsys/pkg/USCODE-2015-title16/html/USCODE-2015-title16-chap5C-subchapI.htm" target="new">16 USC 670a-670f</a>) authorizes the Secretary of Defense to carry out a program to provide for the conservation and rehabilitation of natural resources on military installations. The Act was last reauthorized until 2019 under the <a href="https://www.congress.gov/bill/113th-congress/house-bill/3304" target="new">National Defense Authorization Act of 2014</a>.
