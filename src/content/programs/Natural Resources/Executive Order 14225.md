<b>Immediate Expansion of American Timber Production</b><br>  <i>1 March 2025</i><br>  This EO requires FWS, USFS, and BLM to increase domestic production of timber. The order requires agencies to streamline regulations, identify forest management projects, and to increase the availability of timber resources.
