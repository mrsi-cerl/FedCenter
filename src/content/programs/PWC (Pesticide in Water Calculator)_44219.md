title: PWC (Pesticide in Water Calculator)
---

The latest version of the Pesticide Water Calculator Version 3 (PWC3) simulates pesticide applications to land surfaces and the pesticide's subsequent transport to and fate in water bodies, including surface water bodies as well as simple ground water aquifers. PWC3 is faster and provides greater batch run capabilities than previous versions.
