National Strategy for Electronics Stewardship (PDF), published in 2011, called for a on-line benchmarks of projects that lists each of the projects and the associated tasks. The NSES Benchmarks Progress Report is periodically updated to show the progress and status for each task. Last update March 2015.<p>In July, 2014, the <a href="http://epa.gov/waste/conserve/materials/ecycling/docs/ewaste_task_force_memo.pdf" target="new">Interagency Task Force on Electronics Stewardship</a> released <a href="http://www.epa.gov/epawaste/conserve/materials/ecycling/taskforce/docs/nses_accomplshmnts.pdf" target="new">Moving Sustainable Electronics Forward: An Update to the National Strategy for Electronics Stewardship(PDF)</a>, to highlight the progress made on the National Strategy.
