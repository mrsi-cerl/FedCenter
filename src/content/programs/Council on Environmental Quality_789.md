title: Council on Environmental Quality
---

The Council coordinates federal environmental efforts and works closely with agencies and other White House offices in the development of environmental policies and initiatives. Congress established CEQ within the Executive Office of the President as part of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (<a href="https://ceq.doe.gov/" target="new">NEPA</a>). Additional responsibilities were provided by the Environmental Quality Improvement Act of 1970.
