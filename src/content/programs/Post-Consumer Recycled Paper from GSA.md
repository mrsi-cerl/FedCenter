Federal agencies can purchase copier paper containing 30% postconsumer fiber from the General Services Administration's Schedule 75, Part XI. The Schedule information is on GSA Advantage, found at <a href="https://www.gsaadvantage.gov/">https://www.gsaadvantage.gov/</a>.
