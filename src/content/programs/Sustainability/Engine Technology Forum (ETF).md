title: Engine Technology Forum (ETF)

The Engine Technology Forum (ETF) is the nation's leading advocate for advanced engine technologies, which power critical sectors of the global economy and remain the technology of choice for freight transportation, agriculture, construction, and industrial applications. ETF represents global leaders in internal combustion engine manufacturing and supply, as well as producers of petroleum, renewable, and emerging fuels. Through research, education, and outreach, ETF works to advance understanding of the benefits of advanced engines and the fuels they use, highlighting their contributions to economic growth, environmental progress, and long-term sustainability.
