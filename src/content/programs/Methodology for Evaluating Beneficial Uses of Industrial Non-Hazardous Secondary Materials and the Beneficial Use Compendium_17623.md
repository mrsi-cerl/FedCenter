The Methodology for Evaluating Beneficial Uses of Industrial Non-Hazardous Secondary Materials presents EPA's approach for evaluating a wide range of industrial non-hazardous secondary materials and their associated beneficial uses. Use of the methodology is voluntary and does not change or substitute for existing laws, regulations, or any beneficial use determination(s) that govern the management of individual wastes on either a federal or state level.
