title: Lessons from the Field with Ohio EPA's Compliance Assistance Unit (Web-based)
---

Ohio EPA's Wastewater Compliance Assistance Unit (CAU) works with operators and wastewater treatment staff to identify performance-limiting factors and implement cost-effective solutions that help facilities achieve compliance. This webinar will provide attendees with an understanding of the CAU's process, as well as examples of issues identified and corrective actions taken to return facilities back into compliance.
