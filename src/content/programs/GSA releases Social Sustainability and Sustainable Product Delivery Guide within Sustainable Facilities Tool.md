The SFTool's Social Sustainability module is now live! The pages cover Social Sustainability as it pertains to workers' rights, safe working conditions, prevention of human trafficking, and other human rights issues. The module includes pre- and post-award best practices, resources for the acquisition workforce to verify contractual sustainable delivery of products and services, and a multitude of initiatives, guidelines, standards and tools from across the globe.
