title: Green Building Certification System Review (2012)
---

GSA completed its most recent evaluation of green building certification systems in March 2012.  This evaluation includes certification systems for new construction, major renovations and existing buildings. Section 436(h) of the Energy Independence and Security Act requires GSA to evaluate green building certification systems every five years to identify a system and certification level deemed to be most likely to encourage a comprehensive and environmentally sound approach to certification of green buildings and to provide the findings to the Secretary of Energy who, in consultation with the Department of Defense and GSA, formally identifies the system(s) to be used across the federal government.  GSA is convening key federal portfolio holders in an ad hoc review group co-chaired by GSA, DOD, and DOE to use this recent evaluation and make a formal recommendation on the role of green building certification systems in advancing high performance in the federal real estate portfolio.
