title: Handbook for Planning and Conducting Charrettes for High Performance Projects
---

Building owners, architects, and engineers can use the <a href="http://www.fhwa.dot.gov/reports/pittd/charrett.htm" target="new">charrette process</a> to save time and money by identifying and solving design problems before design and construction begin. A charrette is an intensive workshop in which various stakeholders and experts are brought together to address a particular design project. It is the mechanism that starts the communication process among the project team members, building users, and project management staff.
