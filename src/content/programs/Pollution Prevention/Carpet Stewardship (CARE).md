title: Carpet Stewardship (CARE)
---

The mission of CARE is to advance market-based solutions that increase landfill diversion and recycling of post-consumer carpet, encourage design for recyclability and meet meaningful goals as approved by the CARE Board of Directors.  An annual report is available.  This is a self-regulatory body.
