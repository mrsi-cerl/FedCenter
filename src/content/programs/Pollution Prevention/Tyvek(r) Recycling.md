DuPont Personal Protection offers a free-of-charge recycling program to help customers manage used Tyvek® and IsoClean® protective apparel.      The program applies to garments and accessories that have not been exposed to hazardous materials/dangerous goods (as defined by local, federal or international transportation regulations).  Recycling used Tyvek® reduces overall waste landfill, helps meet corporate solid waste reduction goals and extends the life of valuable materials into new applications.   The program applies to all customers in the continental United States.      To learn more about this program, please contact North America customer service at 1-800-931-3456.
